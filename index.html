📜 About Gandhiji

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, known as Bapu, was born on 2nd October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat. He is known as Father of the Nation. He fought for truth, non-violence and freedom.

🎓 Education & South Africa

Gandhiji went to London in 1888 to study law. In 1893 he went to South Africa. There he faced racism. He started his first Satyagraha there and fought for 21 years for Indians rights.

🕊️ His Main Teachings

  • Truth (Satya): Always speak truth. Truth is God.
  • Non-Violence (Ahimsa): Never hurt anyone.
  • Satyagraha: Fight with truth, not weapons.
  • Swadeshi: Use Indian products, Charkha and Khadi.
  • Simple Living: Live simple, be self-dependent.

🇮🇳 Role in India's Freedom

1. Champaran Movement (1917)

For farmers of Bihar. First Satyagraha in India.

2. Non-Cooperation (1920)

Boycott of British goods, schools and courts.

3. Dandi March (1930)

240 miles walk to make salt and break British law.

4. Quit India (1942)

Slogan 'Do or Die'. Last movement for freedom.

Conclusion & Last Days

He was assassinated on 30th January 1948. But he is still alive in our hearts. His principles of truth and non-violence are relevant even today. Jai Hind!
